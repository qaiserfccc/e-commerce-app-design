## Scope and visitor mode
The storefront home page is a persuade-and-browse surface: help shoppers understand that the site is a contact-lens catalog and move into published listings.

## Audience and task
Shoppers compare only product names, categories, prices, availability, and lens parameters entered in the catalog. The primary action is to browse products or open a listing.

## Proof and constraints
Product details must come from published database records. Do not invent product claims or specifications. Imported listings remain drafts until an operator verifies them. The in-page bag is browse-only; checkout is unavailable.

## Direction and memorable moment
The chromatic specimen index uses ink-blue fields, chartreuse indexing, cool leaf-white surfaces, compressed display type, and compact measurement labels. Its split opening pairs a clear catalog proposition with an explicitly abstract optical study, then leads into unboxed product records and an evidence-based listing guide.

## Unresolved decisions
The operator must verify imported product details and decide payment, delivery, tax, and checkout behavior before orders can be accepted.
